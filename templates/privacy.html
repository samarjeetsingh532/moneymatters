Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly to track your finances, we collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, to help keep the Service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns of your spending. We may also use your email address to send you important account or service-related notices. We do not use your expense data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting or infrastructure, who process data only as necessary to provide those services and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share your individual expense records with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page, or by emailing our support team directly.
